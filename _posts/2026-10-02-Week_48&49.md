### Accumulating all trades as one may have been a mistake

Looking back at it now, accumulating all signals under one group probably was not the best way to find patterns. Mostly because each params have their own sort of identity or grouping separate from others. One group may have performed better with lower RSI than higher RSI. And I have no one in discerning which params is which.

So as I continued further into the project with a new run sweep, but this time without `VIX signal` within `Bear signal`, I will create dedicated groups with their own identities, and compare them that way.
