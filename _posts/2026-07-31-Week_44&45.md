### Complicated exit strategies don't always work
You might think that adding specific rules for either exits, holds, delayed tracking, changing rules when threshold is reached etc. etc. could help with retaining the strong stock growths, whilst dropping unprofitable ones - unfortunately sometimes simple is best.

As adding more convoluted and complex strategies can just as easily pull in the bad tickers in with the good tickers, leading to often worse performance. Here for example is one where a signal is triggered, we track the entry exit logic strategy in the stock, but delay our entry until a certain number of days is reached.

<img width="1590" height="522" alt="cf886b55-7728-4076-9ed6-38ecbb377dd8" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f733c7ce-50b4-4980-9a00-e467ba65e1fc" />

Performance immediately falls when entered from a signal - and it does climb back up, but at that point, the data points left are way too few to be usable, especially when using the large data batches, which actually showed even worse results when entered into the stock longer.

### High VIX, or High % change in VIX is proportional to performance

Next, I checked how each signal values change based on the bracket performance of the tickers, from 1 being worst, to 10 being best. All signals provided zero clear lines of momentum except for VIX value, and VIX % change.

<img width="1486" height="1180" alt="3d5502dc-bc00-4796-aa33-8261ecc71e70" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/502a5c76-d602-4d59-8b3c-7d5cb3941232" />

VIX % change was especially useful, since it removes the regime specificity of a VIX value, and can be applied at any state of the VIX value. Plus, the % change remains consistent when checking present day, to any past days such as 2 or 10 trading days before.

### Trade signal peaks

A concerning problem is that all our signal extractors exhibit a peak somewhere. Most notable, many being on the mid January 2022, the start of the data set. The other thing is the peak is also notable to occur in any crashes like the Trump Tariffs. This leads to inaccurate results, because all the winning trades exists in this one sliver of time, and is impossible to be trading that many capital in that one day.

<img width="1390" height="590" alt="21c031ac-e173-4c77-b1e7-9e3457a680a5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/49a047e8-8888-4ee6-852e-ddeff684223a" />

My first thought is maybe it has something to do with the trading indicators, such as calculating EMA data or something, leading to false data trigger. Especially since I set the start of data signal check from 01/01/2022, not mid January.
