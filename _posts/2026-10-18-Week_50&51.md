### Testing active batches of max or 10

So the thing is, a lot of the signals co-occur on the next following days, meaning we often miss out on some of the allotted signals, which are already limited as is. So I thought whether expanding it to a limit of 10 tickers traded at any one time would increase performance. This does mean each trade instead of 25% for 4, is now 10% for 10. In addition, each trade has a theoretical 4-day trade length based on the averages above.

Example of performance:

```python
# 4: Average return        : 4.2197% | Win rate              : 75.3012% | Total Trade Count     : 166
4A: Average return        : 4.9632% | Win rate              : 80.0000% | Total Trade Count     : 85
    0.3596(1.049632 - 0.008)^(85/4) = -14.44% Loss
4B:  Average return        : 4.0874% | Win rate              : 75.1724% | Total Trade Count     : 145
    0.3596(1.040874 - 0.02)^(145/10) = -51.48% Loss
```

Out of all the tests, max search, all outperformed the flat `10` - because of capital utilization capacity, that the 10 stood no chance for. Plus the higher fees overall due to splitting money into thinner groups.

> Side note, calculation of the Loss is also wrong - I tried factoring in the loss from NZD/USD conversion, every time I am pulling money out of PIE into Sharesies. However I accidentally counted ALL conversion periods from 1990 to present day, instead of 2021 to present day. Recalculating, actually shows return `4A` having more than doubled its capital alone.
